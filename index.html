FAQ

Common questions

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are a USDOT-licensed carrier, fully insured, and bonded. Certificates of insurance are available on request.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead, especially for weekends and the end of the month. Last-minute moves are sometimes available.

Do you offer free estimates?

Absolutely. Estimates are free and come with no obligation. We can do an in-home or video walkthrough for an exact price.

What if something gets damaged?

Every move includes basic liability coverage. You can upgrade to full-value protection for complete peace of mind. We handle claims promptly and fairly.

Can you move a piano or heavy safe?

Yes. Our specialty team has the equipment and experience to move pianos, safes, and other heavy or delicate items safely.
